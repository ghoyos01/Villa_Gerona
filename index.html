The Cabin

Built by hand, kept simple on purpose.

Hollow Pine was built board by board over two seasons, using timber cleared from the property itself. It's been kept deliberately unfussy — thick walls, a big stone hearth, and windows placed for the view, not the floor plan.

There's no television and the cell signal is thin by design. What you'll find instead: a stocked kitchen, a record player, a porch that catches the evening light, and a creek a five-minute walk from the door.
