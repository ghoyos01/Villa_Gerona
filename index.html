The House

Built for the countryside, finished like a home.

Villa Gerona is a rural tourist country house set in the green hills along Vía a La Buitrera, just outside Cali. Enjoy four levels of comfort, Samsung tech in every room, and top-tier security — a rustic, cozy design with wide open spaces, plenty of natural light, and a decor that invites relaxation.

Every bedroom and open space features large windows fitted with screens (anjeos), letting a gentle breeze move through the house all day. They close fully on cooler nights, keeping things warm and quiet. Inside, every detail was custom-built: paneled interior doors, moisture-resistant carpentry, and quartz countertops chosen for both quality and durability.

Guest Access

The entire property, privately yours.

Guests have full, exclusive access to the entire property for the duration of their stay — every bedroom, bathroom, the living area, and the fully equipped kitchen, plus the private outdoor spaces: gardens, BBQ station, and lounge areas. Secure parking inside the gated estate fits up to 3 vehicles.

A note on privacy
A maintenance staff member may briefly access the gardens or exterior at pre-scheduled times to keep the grounds beautiful — they will never enter the house without your permission.
